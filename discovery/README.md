# Discovery Pack: Palmstone Living

Everything the client has given you. This is where Stage 1 starts. Do not edit these files; they are your source material. Copy anything you want to change.

Palmstone Living is a small online homewares store in Melbourne, run by four people. They sell through their own website, a large online marketplace, and Instagram direct messages. All their work lives in separate Google Sheets that different people keep in different ways. Management cannot see the whole business in one place, so decisions come late.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `call-transcript.md` | Your 20-minute discovery call with the owner and the operations manager |
| `follow-up-email.md` | The owner's reply to your first round of questions |
| `warehouse-note.md` | A message from the part-time warehouse person |
| `sheets/website-orders-2026.csv` | Website orders, exported from Google Sheets |
| `sheets/marketplace-orders-q3.csv` | Marketplace orders, downloaded from the marketplace and pasted into a sheet |
| `sheets/instagram-orders.csv` | Instagram orders, typed in by hand |
| `sheets/inventory-FINAL-v3.csv` | The stock sheet |
| `sheets/marketing-spend.csv` | Weekly advertising spend |
| `sheets/returns-log.csv` | Returns and refunds |

All names, orders and figures are made up. Prices are in Australian dollars.

The `maintenance/` folder at the top of the repository is for Stage 7. Do not open it before then.
