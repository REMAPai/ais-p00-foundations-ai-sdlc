# Discovery Call: Palmstone Living

**Attendees:** Dana (owner), Sam (operations manager), you
**Length:** about 20 minutes. Transcribed automatically, lightly tidied.

---

**You:** Thanks for making the time. Can you start by telling me how the business runs day to day?

**Dana:** Sure. We sell homewares. Throws, candles, mugs, lamp shades, that kind of thing. About twelve products right now. We sell on our own website, on the marketplace, and through Instagram, where people just message us.

**Sam:** And every one of those lives in its own Google Sheet. The website one is fine, mostly. The marketplace one I download and paste in. Instagram, Riley types those in by hand when someone orders.

**You:** Who else touches the sheets?

**Sam:** Riley does marketing, part-time, and keeps the ad spend sheet. Jules does the warehouse two days a week and keeps the stock sheet. There's a returns sheet I look after. So six sheets, four people.

**You:** What happens when you need to know how the business is doing?

**Dana:** That's the problem. Every Monday we have a catch-up, and Sam spends the whole morning copying numbers out of all the sheets into a summary. And the numbers never quite match.

**Sam:** Half a day, every week. And by the time we look at it, it's already old.

**You:** Can you give me an example of a decision that came too late?

**Dana:** The lamp shades. We ran a big ad push in August, spent good money, and it turned out we were nearly out of stock. Nobody saw it because the stock sheet is in one place and the ads are in another. And I have a feeling the lamp shades come back a lot as well, but I honestly can't tell you.

**Sam:** We also don't really know which channel makes us money. The marketplace sells a lot, but they take a fee, and we pay for ads. Is it actually worth it? No idea.

**You:** If you had one screen, what would you want to see on it?

**Dana:** Sales, how much we're making, by channel. Our best and worst products. What's about to run out. And whether the ads are paying for themselves.

**Sam:** Returns too. Which products come back and why.

**Dana:** And compared to last week, so I can see if things are going up or down.

**You:** When you say "sales", what do you mean exactly? What the customer paid, or what you keep?

**Dana:** Just sales. The total. I mean, I suppose what we keep matters more. Let me think about that.

**You:** Fair. Do you need to see individual orders, or just the totals?

**Dana:** I just need the big numbers. I don't have time to dig.

**Sam:** I'd want to click into it, though. When a number looks wrong I need to see which orders make it up.

**You:** How often would it need updating?

**Sam:** Whenever. Weekly at least. Eventually it would be great if it just pulled straight from the Google Sheets, but I know that might be a bigger job.

**Dana:** One more thing. I sometimes show the numbers to the bank, or to our accountant. I don't want customer names on the screen when I do that.

**You:** Understood. Where would you look at it? Laptop, phone?

**Dana:** Laptop on Mondays. Phone would be nice when I'm out.

**You:** Anything you've tried before?

**Sam:** I built a summary tab once with formulas pulling from the other sheets. It broke the first time someone added a column. Nobody trusts it now.

**You:** That's really helpful. I'll send a few follow-up questions by email, then come back with a plan and a first look at the design.

**Dana:** Perfect. Honestly, if Sam gets their Monday back, that alone is worth it.
