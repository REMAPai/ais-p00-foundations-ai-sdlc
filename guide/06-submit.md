# 6. Submit and Present

## Submitting (Day 5)

1. Check every box in [SUCCESS.md](../SUCCESS.md).
2. Fill in `SUBMISSION.md`.
3. On the platform, submit your repository URL, the commit you want scored, and your Loom link.

If anything is missing, the AI check sends it back with a list. Fix it and resubmit.

## The Loom (8 minutes or less, all seven stages)

Show your real files and your running app, not slides. About one minute per stage.

| Time | Cover |
|---|---|
| 0:00 to 0:30 | What you built, for whom, and the problem it solves |
| About 1:00 each | Each of the seven stages: what you produced, what AI did, and one decision you made yourself |
| Inside Stage 5 | Your tests running, including one Playwright test in the browser |
| Last 0:30 | Your AI pipeline in one line, and one token habit you will keep |

## The Live Review (about 30 minutes)

Your reviewer schedules it after the AI check passes.

- You walk through your dashboard and one stage of your choice in five minutes.
- They pick a piece of your code and ask you to explain it.
- They ask you to run your tests, then change something and run them again.
- They ask questions from the list below.

## Gap-Check Questions

Prepare for all of them.

1. Walk me through the seven stages in two minutes. Which one did you rush, and what did it cost you?
2. Pick one requirement. Which source did it come from, and what is its acceptance criterion?
3. Dana said "sales" on the call and something more specific in the email. Which did you build, and where does it show?
4. What problems did you find in the sheets? Which one was hardest to clean, and what happens to a row you cannot clean?
5. Where did two sources disagree, and how did you decide?
6. Name one thing the AI got wrong in discovery or design. How did you catch it?
7. Why did you choose this folder structure and this data model?
8. What is the difference between a unit test, an integration test and a Playwright test? Show me one of each in your repository.
9. How do you know your tests actually test something?
10. What is regression testing, and when did you run it?
11. What did your UAT find?
12. What would happen if someone pushed code that broke a test?
13. Where are your secrets, and how do you know none are in Git?
14. Walk me through your bug fix. Why did you write the test first?
15. What is in your `CLAUDE.md`, and what did you add after the AI made a mistake?
16. Which habit saved you the most tokens?
17. Explain this piece of code (the reviewer picks it).

## Deadline

5 working days from the day you create your copy. The platform reminds you and your assigner. A late submission is flagged and kept on record, nothing more. If you will miss it, say so early.

## Portfolio Use

Your public repository is your portfolio piece. Present your dashboard and write-ups as your own practice work.
